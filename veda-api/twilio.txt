1. TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SIDThis is your main account ID.Log in to your Twilio Console.On the Dashboard (Console Home), look under the "Account Info" section.You will see your Account SID. It starts with AC.2. TWILIO_API_KEY & TWILIO_API_SECRETFor security, we don't use your main Auth Token in the code; we use an API Key.Go to Account (top right) $\rightarrow$ API Keys & Tokens.Click Create API Key.Give it a name (e.g., "VEDA_ERP_APP").Choose Standard as the key type.IMPORTANT: Twilio will show you the SID (this is your TWILIO_API_KEY, starts with SK) and the Secret (this is your TWILIO_API_SECRET).Copy the Secret immediately; it will never be shown again!3. TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBERThis is the number patients will see on their caller ID.In the left sidebar, go to Phone Numbers $\rightarrow$ Manage $\rightarrow$ Buy a Number.Search for a number in your desired country.CRITICAL: Make sure the number has the "Voice" capability (look for the phone icon).Once purchased, copy the full number in E.164 format (e.g., +91700XXXXXXX).4. TWILIO_TWIML_APP_SIDThis is the "Brain" that connects your React frontend to your NestJS backend.Go to Voice $\rightarrow$ Manage $\rightarrow$ TwiML Apps.Click Create new TwiML App.Name: VEDA ERP Bridge.Voice Configuration:Request URL: Enter your backend URL followed by /calls/bridge (e.g., https://your-api.render.com/calls/bridge).HTTP Method: Set this to POST.Click Save.After saving, you will see a unique SID starting with AP. This is your TWILIO_TWIML_APP_SID.
